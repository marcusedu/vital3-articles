# How to Reduce Screen Time and Regain Focus

The smartphone is one of the most powerful tools ever created, but it can also be the biggest source of distraction in your day. When you open your phone without a clear objective, your brain seeks quick dopamine in social media and notifications.

---

## 1. The Concept of Intentional Friction

To master phone usage, you don't need to become a monk. You need to apply **intentional friction**:
- Disable non-essential notifications.
- Remove social media apps from your home screen.
- Set up native daily limits on your operating system.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step by Platform

### On Android (Digital Wellbeing):
1. Access the **Settings** menu on your Android.
2. Select **Digital Wellbeing & parental controls**.
3. Set an **App Timer** for social media apps like Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube.

### On iPhone / iOS (Screen Time):
1. Open the iPhone **Settings**.
2. Tap on **Screen Time**.
3. Enable the option and choose **App Limits** to define daily usage quotas.

## 4. The 15-Minute Strategy

Before unlocking your phone, ask yourself a simple question:
> *"What exactly did I come here to do?"*

If the answer is "nothing", put the phone away and focus on your 3 vital tasks of the day!
